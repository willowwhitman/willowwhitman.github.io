The term

What Milgram meant by the agentic state

Milgram's term for the felt shift in which a person stops experiencing themselves as the author of an act and begins experiencing themselves as the instrument of someone else's decision. Guilt has nowhere to attach because the self that could refuse has, for the duration, been handed over.

That is how The Machinery of Compliance defines it — a free, source-checked book on coercive control by Willow Whitman, published here in full with its notes.
